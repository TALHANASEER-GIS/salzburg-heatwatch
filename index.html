METHODS & SOURCES

Real observations.
Transparent limitations.

This dashboard explores surface heat and vegetation across Salzburg and Wals-Siezenheim. It is an exploratory portfolio analysis, not a human-health heat-risk assessment.

Monthly observations: January–December 2025

For each indicator, overlapping observations on the same date are reduced to a daily median. Monthly pixel values are medians of valid daily medians. A pixel needs at least two valid acquisition days. Counts are distinct valid days at a pixel, rather than scene counts. The inspector reports an area-weighted mean of those counts over qualifying pixels. The satellite dates contributing to temperature and NDVI can differ.

Temperature

Landsat 8 and 9 Collection 2 Level-2 ST_B10, scaled to Celsius. Quality flags mask fill, clouds, shadows, cirrus, snow and saturation. The 30 m export grid is finer than the effective thermal information. Values describe available clear, snow-free daytime observations, not all-weather monthly means or air temperature.

Vegetation

Sentinel-2 harmonized Level-2A NDVI: (B8 − B4) / (B8 + B4). SCL classes 4, 5 and 6 are retained. Cloud-mask errors can remain. Snow is excluded, so winter coverage can be very low. NDVI is not tree shade or public green-space access.

Grid, filters and low coverage

100 m squares aligned in EPSG:25833, clipped separately to each municipality. Boundary squares may have two municipality parts. Means use exported pixel overlap areas, excluding missing values. Summary cards weight cells by valid indicator area. The 80% cell filter applies only to the active indicator. Coverage card and quality flags always refer to the whole municipality before that filter. A representative-area mean and annual trend point are withheld below 50% valid municipality coverage. These 80% and 50% cutoffs are illustrative screening rules, not validated scientific thresholds. Local cell values and distributions remain inspectable. Trend values use all eligible observed area, before cell filtering. Map panning does not change statistics.

Original single-scene view

The optional original snapshot uses Landsat 9 on 25 June 2025 and NDVI from 18 June–2 July 2025. It is separate from the June monthly composite. Its original provenance remains available in the source metadata download.

Boundaries and scope

Uploaded Statistik Austria municipality dataset labelled 2025. Municipality IDs: 50101 and 50338. Confirm its reference date against the original download. No park distance, walking access, population exposure or priority index has been calculated.
